MOHAMMAD ZIMOffensive Security · Bug Bounty · Open Source

I'm Mohammad Zim, known online as Mr-Destroyer. What started as curiosity about how websites work became a craft: building offensive security tools, hunting bugs, and studying the cracks in every system.

My philosophy is simple — security tools should be fast, reliable, and built for real workflows: payload generation, endpoint hunting, credential discovery, exploitation. Everything I ship is free and open-source.

And I don't gatekeep. On my channel ZimShell I break down tools, techniques, and CVEs so the next hacker learns faster than I did. Hack the planet — then teach it.
